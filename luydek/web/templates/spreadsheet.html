Count and edit in Excel

  • Count: on the Count sheet, write what you found in Counted. Leave it empty for what you didn't count: it stays as it is. 0 means none were there.
  • One product, several dates: each date is its own row. Count each one.
  • Wrong date or place? Fix Expiry, Where or Lot on its row.
  • Found something LuyDek doesn't know about? Add a row at the bottom of Count: product, where, expiry, how many.
  • Product details: change the yellow columns on Stock (name, supplier, case size, cost, barcode, must-have). A row with no ID adds a product.
Count and upload on the same day: sales after you count would be undone by the count.
