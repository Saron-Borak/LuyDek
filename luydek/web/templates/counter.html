{{ _("Camera setup") }}
  1. Point the camera straight down at a plain, matte mat
    Fill the picture with the mat: move the camera, or zoom by pinching the picture or with the 1× / 2× buttons on it. Changing the zoom later means calibrating again.
  2. Clear the mat, then calibrate
  3. Mark the mat, so hands and things beside it never count
    Drag a box on the picture from one corner of the mat to the other. Only items inside it count.
  4. Pharmacies only: mark the tablet tray (optional)
    Drag a box around the small black card where loose tablets are put down. Calibrate with the card empty. When selling loose, put the box the tablets came from beside the card.
